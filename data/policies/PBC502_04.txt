POLICY: PBC502.04 - SJ AR Students Receivables and Aged Receivables
Revised: May 2024
By: John Hardin

---

SECTION 1: Generate AR Receivable/Invoice Process

The debt represents tuition, mandatory campus fees, housing, and bookstore charges.
Charges are initiated by the student when they register for classes through their MySJSU portal.
The Housing department posts housing charges to student accounts. All charges can be viewed through MySJSU.

SJSU does not invoice students for registration charges. AR receivable is created when a student
registers for courses and students are responsible for viewing their account on MySJSU for charges
and payment due date(s).

While SJSU does not invoice students, the Bursar's Office sends out multiple payment reminder
messages via the PeopleSoft (PS) Messaging process starting approximately seven and two days prior
to each due date to remind students with balances that they have a balance due.

The messaging will NOT include students who have a fee deferral. They may have a fee deferral from:
- The Financial Aid and Scholarship Office because they are receiving financial aid, OR
- The Bursar's Office because they are receiving a waiver or sponsorship.

Payments can be made:
- Online (preferred method)
- In-person (cash or checks)
- By mail
- Overnight drop box at the Bursar's Office (checks only)

The Bursar's Office oversees the collection of registration and housing charges debt.

---

SECTION 2: Holds - Conditions and Thresholds

Holds are automatically placed by PeopleSoft (PS) on University records beginning the first day
the balance is past due.

Hold thresholds:
- For balances greater than $2.00 and less than $350.00: a service indicator is placed restricting DIPLOMAS only.
- For balances of $350.00 or greater: a service indicator is placed restricting REGISTRATION AND DIPLOMAS.

---

SECTION 3: Aged Receivables Collection Procedures - Due Diligence

The Bursar's Office performs timely collection efforts to resolve aged receivables, in order to
comply with due diligence per Federal, State, and University guidelines.

Student Account Specialists work the monthly receivables reports that show account balances that
are 61+ days delinquent. Accounts are divided by alphabetic groups between the Student Account Specialists.

Collection contact timelines:
- 61-90 days past due: First collection notice sent (CL1)
- 91-120 days past due: Second collection notice sent (CL2)
- 121-150 days past due: Final demand notice sent — warns account will go to collection agency if not paid

These collection contacts/messages are sent to students via:
- PeopleSoft Messaging System
- Email
- Paper letter via USPS
- Phone calls

---

SECTION 4: Collection Agency Referral and Fees

A $20.00 Administrative Fee (late fee) is charged to the account BEFORE the account is referred
to an outside collection agency.

The Student Account Specialist submits an adjustment sheet internally to the Associate Bursar for
Student Accts/Collections for approval to have the $20.00 charge added to the student's account
prior to sending the account to a collection agency.

Accounts are referred to a collection agency after the add/drop period of the following term, if:
- There is no response to collection efforts, AND
- The third past due notice (final demand) has been sent.

When an account is sent to a collection agency, a BO4 (Departmental Hold/COLL) service indicator
is added in PeopleSoft.

Accounts are referred to a collection agency for one year. If attempts with that agency are
unsuccessful or no assets found, the account is returned and referred to a second collection agency.

---

SECTION 5: Franchise Tax Board (FTB) Submission

Accounts are also submitted to the Franchise Tax Board (FTB) for offsetting of State tax refunds.

Conditions for FTB submission:
- Occurs yearly in November
- For past due debts of 100 days or more
- Does NOT include current term outstanding balance for enrolled students
- Balance must be $50.00 or greater

---

SECTION 6: Housing Balances

Student Account Specialists coordinate with Housing on outstanding housing balances, i.e.,
eviction proceedings have been initiated.

Housing generally monitors and performs due diligence on outstanding housing balances while the
student is currently registered. After Housing collection efforts are exhausted, they send a
request to the Bursar's Office to send a final demand letter and the account is referred to a
collection agency as needed.

---

SECTION 7: Uncollectible Accounts (Write-offs)

After three progressive collection calls/messages/letters have been sent, debts that are
151-days delinquent with no payment or promise to pay will be handled as follows:

Write-off for balances $349.99 or less (BO2-SMWOF):
- Student Account Specialist prepares an adjustment sheet after the add/drop period of the following term.
- Submit to Associate Bursar for write-off approval after all in-office due diligence has been met.
- Place a BO2-SMWOF service indicator.
- Enter comments on the PS account including: debt amount written-off, debt type, and term the debt originated.

Write-off for balances $350.00 to $9,999.99 (B06-WROFF):
- Account must be returned from a second collection agency as uncollectible.
- Student Account Specialist prepares a write-off request and submits to Associate Bursar for 1st approval.
- Associate Bursar sends to Sr AVP for Finance and Business Services for final approval.
- A B06-WROFF service indicator is placed on the account.

Write-off for balances $10,000.00 and greater (B06-WROFF):
- Student Account Specialist prepares write-off request.
- Submit to Associate Bursar for 1st approval.
- Associate Bursar sends to Sr AVP for Finance and Business Services for 2nd approval.
- VP of Finance gives final approval.
- A B06-WROFF service indicator is placed on the account.

---

SECTION 8: Service Indicators Reference

B01 - Past due hold restricting diplomas (balance $2.00 - $349.99)
B02 - Past due hold restricting registration and diplomas (balance $350.00+)
BO4 / B04 - Departmental Hold/COLL — placed when account sent to collection agency
B05 - Tuition fee deferral — placed by Bursar, FAO, or Athletics to prevent/remove past due holds
BO2-SMWOF - Write-off indicator for balances under $350
B06-WROFF - Write-off indicator for balances $350 and above

---

SECTION 9: Payment Options

Students can pay their balance through:
- Online (preferred): Credit card, ACH, 529 plan account, international payment via Flywire
- Mail: Personal check, money order, cashier's check, demand draft
- In person: Cash, check, money order, cashier's check, demand draft
- Overnight drop box at the Bursar's Office: Checks only

Online payment portal: MySJSU portal (sjsu.edu/bursar/payment-refunds/pay-now.php)

SECTION 11: Exclusions from Collection Outreach

The following students should NOT receive collection notices:
- Students with a fee deferral from the Financial Aid and Scholarship Office
- Students with a fee deferral from the Bursar's Office (waiver or sponsorship recipients)
- Students with a B05 tuition fee deferral service indicator
